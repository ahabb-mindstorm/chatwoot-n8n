# What does "Tournament Cancelled" mean?

A cancelled tournament states that no results are posted for that tournament and the joining fee is refunded to all of the participants.

A tournament can get cancelled due to detection of fraudulent activities, unfair means or technical issues that may arise during its tenure.

The status of the tournament can be seen in the leaderboard as "Tournament Cancelled".

Our goal is to make sure that the game stands fair and no advantage is given to any player or affect the results of the tournament.
