# Can I use bonus cash to purchase?

Yes, you may use bonus cashto purchase items in the shop, however bonus cash will only be deducted in the absence of cash.

For instance, if you wantto buy an avatar worth $10.00 and have $9.00 cash and $5.00 Bonus cash in your wallet, all of you cash will be deducted and $1 from you bonus cash will be deducted, leavingyou with one avatar and $4.00 Bonus Cash
