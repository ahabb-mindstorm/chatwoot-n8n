# How do multipliers work?

Example:
You enter a $1 tournament:
Base reward: 100 coins + 20 tickets

- Your equipment bonus: +10% Ticket, 11% Coin, 5% Cash

-  You receive:
111 coins → 11 extra coins from equipment bonus

- 22 tickets → 2 extra tickets from equipment bonus

- If you win $2 → $0.10 bonus cash from equipment bonus
