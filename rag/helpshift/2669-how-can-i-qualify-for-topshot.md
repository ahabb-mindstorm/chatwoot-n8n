# How can I qualify for TopShot?

Currently users are eligible for TopShot if at least one of their shots satisfy the following criteria:
User finishes hole on a putt of more than 20 feet or

- User finishes hole on a chip of more than 20 yards (or 60 feet)

-  Keep in mind that a user has to score a par value of 0 or less to satisfy eligibility for TopShot.
In instances where multiple users are eligible for TopShot, then the longest shot from either category will be used to anoint the TopShot award winner.
