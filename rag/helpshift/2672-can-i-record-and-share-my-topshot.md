# Can I record and share my TopShot?

Yes, you can record any shot from tournaments you have won TopShot in, and share it for Bonus Cash (amount varies per tournament). To achieve this, click on any recently concluded tournament where you have won TopShot to claim it. You will be prompted to share for an additional $0.50 Bonus Cash (may be subject to change in future iterations).
You will receive your sharing bonus upon successfully sharing your recording. Any TopShot reward and its associated share reward can only be claimed once.
Keep in mind that we do not record your device, rather we store shot data that we replay on your phone for your recording needs.
