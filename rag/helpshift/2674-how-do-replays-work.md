# How do replays work?

We store every player's shot data, that we then playback when anyone goes to the replay menu. We do not record any video of a player's device, we only rerun the datapoints we store when a player initially takes the shot.
When a player chooses to record their TopShot, the recording will only be saved on the user’s device.
Keep in mind that this may cause a slight discrepancy between the shots you take in game and how they may look in replay. The ball will end up in the same position for each shot, but the path it takes might be slightly different.
