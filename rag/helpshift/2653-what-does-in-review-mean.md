# What does "In Review" mean?

Once all of the players have completed their turn, the status of the tournament changes to "In Review", where the entirety of the gameplay and logs are verified.

The aim is to make sure no fraudulent or unfair means were used to win the tournament.

Therefore, tournament result compilation can take some time.
