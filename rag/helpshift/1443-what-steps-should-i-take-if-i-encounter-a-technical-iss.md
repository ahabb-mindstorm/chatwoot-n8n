# What steps should I take if I encounter a technical issue while playing my game?

If you encounter a technical issue while playing the game, please reach out to our Support team at progolf@mindstormstudios.com.

When reporting the issue, provide as much detail as possible, including the time of occurrence, any error messages encountered, and the sequence of events following the issue.

It's crucial to include the Tournament ID when reporting issues. To find the Tournament ID, navigate to “Results” in the game footer and select the relevant Tournament. The ID is located at the bottom of the leaderboard.

Additionally, you can view the ID during gameplay after completing a hole
