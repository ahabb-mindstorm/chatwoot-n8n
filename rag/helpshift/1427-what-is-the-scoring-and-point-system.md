# What is the scoring and point system?

- Scoring is based on shots taken. The fewer shots you complete your hole in, the higher points you earn.

- You can also earn bonus points by hitting perfect shots and by accurately landing the ball on fairways & green.

- Whoever has the best score and highest points wins.

- In the case of tie-breakers, if two or more players get the same points they will share the same position. The prizes (if there are any) will be combined and divided equally between all players that are tied.

- All points and bonuses are awarded till PAR score only.

- GIR (Green Hits in Regulation) is active when a player lands on green with 2 shots less than par.

- Balls that land on green with GIR receive more bonus points the closer they are to the hole.

- If time runs out, your score will not be counted for that tournament and will be marked as forfeited.

- Landing on different surfaces gives different points. Fairway gives 100, Rough gives 90, Fringe gives 100, Bunker gives 90, Green gives 100 while out of bounds and water hazard does not have any score.

- You receive a GIR bonus for landing the ball close to the pin with GIR. The closer you are the more points you earn. Points range from 0 to 350.

- Green Hits In Regulation is active when a player lands on putting area with 2 shots less than a par score.

- You receive a Putt bonus points which increase with how far you make a PUTT from. Points range from 0 to 350.

- You receive a 10 point Recovery bonus when a recovery is made from Bunker or Rough onto Fairway, Fringe or Green.

- You receive a 10 point Perfect shot bonus if the released shot is perfectly aligned in the middle of the aim target.

- You receive a 5 point Chip bonus whenever a shot is chipped in the hole using any of the clubs.

- You receive a Tee off bonus when taking a tee shot. The further you get the ball, the more points you earn. Points range from 0 to 100.

- For finishing a hole with -3 strokes, you receive 15000 points. For finishing a hole with -2 strokes, you receive 12500 points. For finishing a hole with -1 strokes, you receive 10000 points. For finishing a hole with 0 strokes, you receive 7500 points. For finishing a hole with 1 stroke, you receive 5000 points. For finishing a hole with 2 strokes, you receive 2500 points. For finishing a hole with 3 or more strokes, you receive 0 points.

- For scoring a hole in one, you get 500 extra points.
