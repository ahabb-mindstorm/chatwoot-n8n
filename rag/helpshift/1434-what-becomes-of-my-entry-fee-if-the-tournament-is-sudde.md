# What becomes of my entry fee if the tournament is suddenly disrupted?

In the event that your game is interrupted, your phone turns off, or your connection is lost during a tournament, the game will be automatically paused for the allowable playtime designated for that tournament.

When you resume the game, you can continue from where you left off.

However, if you do not return within the allocated playtime, your accumulated score will be forfeited, resulting in the forfeiture of your entry fee.

Additionally, it's important to note that using the Pause feature does not extend your playtime in any way.
