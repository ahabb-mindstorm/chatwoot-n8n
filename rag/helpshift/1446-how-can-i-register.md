# How can I register?

Once you complete your first deposit through Wallet, a popup will appear requesting your phone number. The request is not mandatory and can be skipped for later. If opted in, an OTP will be sent to the entered number for verification.
Registration is mandatory while doing your first withdrawal, where a popup appears. Fill out your full name, date of birth, email address, and phone number. If you’ve previously not entered your phone number, you will be requested to enter again. The phone number is verified via OTP.
Enter the code you received into the registration form, and your registration will be complete.
