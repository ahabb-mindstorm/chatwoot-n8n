# How will the charge appear on my card statement or PayPal account?

When making a deposit in Pro Golf: Real Cash, you will see the charge reflected on your card or PayPal statement as:
Apple Pay: *Mindstorm Studios LLC or *Pro Golf
PayPal: *Mindstorm Studios LLC or *Pro Golf
