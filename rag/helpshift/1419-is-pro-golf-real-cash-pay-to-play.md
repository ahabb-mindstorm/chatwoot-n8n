# Is Pro Golf: Real Cash pay to play?

No. If you prefer not to deposit, you can visit daily to collect free Coins and participate in our Coins tournaments as often as you wish.
Coins can be used to win Bonus Cash. While Bonus Cash cannot be withdrawn, any winnings obtained using Bonus Cash can be withdrawn.
