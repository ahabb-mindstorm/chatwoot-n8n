# What are Rating Points (RP)?

Rating Points (RP) are tied to each equipped club.
Your total Equipment Rating is the combined RP of all equipped clubs.
Higher Equipment Rating:
Unlocks better Tour Bags

- Improves access to higher rarity equipment

- Increases potential earnings bonuses

-  Upgrading clubs increases their RP.
