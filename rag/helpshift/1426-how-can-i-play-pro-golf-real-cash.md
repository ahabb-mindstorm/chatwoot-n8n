# How can I play Pro Golf: Real Cash?

Here's how it works:

- There are multiple tournament types from which you can choose from.

- A tournament entry fee is either coin, cash, rewarded video or a particular equipment.

- If you do not have the required amount, you can load cash into your wallet and can also receive free coins with most offers.

- Each tournament has a set of conditions. The course holes can be prefixed or random.

- Tee spot, needle speed and wind conditions are also prefixed or random.

- A certain number of players participate in a single tournament.

- Every tournament has a certain time limit which is based on the type of tournament, the number of holes and the number of players within.

- Every hole has a designated Par value which corresponds to the length of the hole.

- Finish the hole with the minimum number of shots.

- Finish in one of the top places on the leaderboard. After all players have finished playing, win a prize based on your position.

- All equipment (clubs, balls & tees) in the game behaves exactly the same and are only cosmetically different to ensure fair and skill based gameplay.
