# How do I report an issue with payments?

If you experience an issue with any of your payments, in regards to refunds or deposits, please contact our support team at progolf@mindstormstudios.com.
Please describe the issue in detail and include as much information as possible regarding the payment you experienced the issue with.
Rest assured that our Support Team is here to help.
