# Can I get lootbags from playing tournaments?

Yes, unless the tournament does not feature a lootbag icon in the UI, you may get lootbags upon successfully completing a tournament.

Note that you may have to play the same tournament to completion upto 3 times depending on the completions required to get your lootbag.
