# How do I qualify for a Championship?

Qualification in a tournament is contingent on collecting a minimum amount of tickets set for that tournament. Ticket requirements may vary across tournaments. You can earn tickets by playing any cash or ads tournament, with higher stakes tournaments paying out more tickets. You can view the exact amount of tickets required in the individual championship's section in the seasons tab in the game.
