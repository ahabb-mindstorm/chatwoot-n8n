# How do I request a withdrawal?

Simply follow the following steps:
At the bottom of the screen, in the footer, tap on "Wallet".
- Select the “Withdraw” tab at the top of the Wallet.
- To see the balance available to withdraw, take a look at “Withdrawable Balance”.
- Enter the amount you wish to withdraw in the “Amount to Withdraw” field.
- Enter your PayPal email address – the email address that is connected to your account, not the address that starts with PayPal.me/ – if you enter that address it will not submit the request.
- Review your request to make sure it’s correct, then press "Submit Withdrawal".
-  Once you’ve completed the steps, we will notify you via email that your request has been received with the withdrawal reference number. When your withdrawal processing is complete, you will receive a confirmation email as well.
A couple of things to keep in mind when submitting a withdrawal request:
The minimum amount required in order to submit a withdrawal request is $5.00.
- There is a $1.00 withdrawal processing fee for each withdrawal request.
- Withdrawals may be processed as refunds of your original deposits back to your original method of payment used in the game.
- Withdrawals are processed within 14 days, but usually, players receive the funds within 2 business days. Please be sure to read your withdrawal confirmation email for more information.
- Only one withdrawal can be requested at a time.
-
We may need you to do ID verification, meaning you may have to submit your SSN or equivalent to process your withdrawal. ID verification is only required once, and users who have verified their identity can process further withdrawals without submitting ID.
Note:
When submitting a withdrawal request, the funds you requested will be deducted from your current balance along with any Bonus Cash remaining in your account. At the time of the withdrawal request, all Bonus Cash will be forfeited. The remaining funds in your balance are withdrawable funds that can be used to continue playing.
