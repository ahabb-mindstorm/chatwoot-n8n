# How can I see tournament results?

When participating in a tournament, you might notice that other players are still actively playing because not everyone starts at the same time. Tournaments close once enough players have joined and finished their games. You will only view the final results after all participants have completed their matches.

Tournaments also have a fixed duration and conclude when the allotted time expires, regardless of whether all players have finished. During this time, the leaderboard will indicate "Results Pending", indicating that some players have yet to complete their games. Once the tournament concludes, you will see your final rank and can collect any winnings you may have earned.

If you have enabled push notifications, you will receive a notification on your phone informing you that the results are available.

Please note that the duration for tournaments to finish may vary depending on the number of participants involved.
