# Can I use multiple payment methods on my account?

Yes, you can use either Apple Pay or PayPal payment to make a deposit.
If you had previously made a deposit with us, follow the steps below to choose a new payment method to make your deposit.
To get started, tap the Wallet icon in the footer or the "+" icon in the top right-hand corner, right next to your cash balance.
- Choose the desired amount you would like to deposit.
- Choose the payment method you wish to use to make the deposit: Apple Pay or PayPal, and enter your details to complete the deposit.
-  As soon as your payment is approved, the funds are immediately credited to your in-game balance for you to play.
