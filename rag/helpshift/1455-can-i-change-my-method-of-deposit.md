# Can I change my method of deposit?

Yes, you can change your payment method at any time.
To do that, tap the wallet icon or the deposit icon in the game menu bar, which can be found in the footer. Then, click on the desired deposit amount you would like to add.
Choose the payment method you wish to check out with (either PayPal or Apple Pay), enter your details, and complete the purchase.
