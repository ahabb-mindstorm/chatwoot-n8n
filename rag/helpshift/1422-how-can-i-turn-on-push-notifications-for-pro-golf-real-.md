# How can I turn on push notifications for Pro Golf: Real Cash?

To ensure you stay in the loop of our new content and fun activities, simply follow the steps below on how to turn your notifications on for Pro Golf: Real Cash.

For Apple Devices:

Step 1: Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad.

Step 2: Tap on “Notifications”

Step 3: Scroll down and locate the “Pro Golf: Real Cash” app.

Step 4: Turn on the “Allow Notifications” switch.
