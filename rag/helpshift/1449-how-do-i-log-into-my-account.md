# How do I log into my account?

You are automatically logged in the first time you install and play Pro Golf: Real Cash. The account information is linked to your device.

If your app gets uninstalled by mistake or on purpose, your account information is stored with a reference to the device you were playing with. As you reinstall the game on the same device you will be automatically logged in and can start playing at any time!
