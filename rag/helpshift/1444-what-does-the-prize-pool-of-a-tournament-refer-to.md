# What does the prize pool of a tournament refer to?

Each tournament features a unique prize pool composed of the total possible winnings available for that particular event.
For example, in a tournament with three winners, the first, second, and third places receive portions of the total prize pool.
The first-place winner typically receives a larger reward compared to the second and third places.
It's important to note that no single player receives the entire prize pool alone.

For instance, in the "PGX Champions" tournament, the prize pool is $20. The specific prizes awarded for each placement are as follows:

- 1st: $12

- 2nd: $6

- 3rd: $2

To check the prize pool and placement prizes, you may tap on the play button of a tournament and inside the detail screen, open the prizes tab.
