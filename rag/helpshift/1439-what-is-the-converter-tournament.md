# What is the Converter tournament?

In the Converter tournament, participants compete with Coins to win Bonus Cash, which sets it apart from all other tournaments.

This Bonus Cash can then be used to enter more challenging tournaments.

This approach ensures that everyone has the opportunity to compete for cash prizes.

Please be aware that this tournament is only available in supported locations as it involves real-money participation.
