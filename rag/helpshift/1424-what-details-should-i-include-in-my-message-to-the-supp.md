# What details should I include in my message to the Support team?

If you are sending the Support team a message, please try to describe your issue in as much detail as possible. Any information you provide will aid the support team in resolving your issue quicker and more efficiently.

If you are contacting the Support team in regards to a payment issue, be sure to include the date and amount of the payment, and which payment method was used. (Apple Pay or PayPal email address).

If you are contacting the Support team in regards to a tournament issue, be sure to include a screenshot of the leaderboard results, making sure the tournament ID is displayed.

If any of the necessary information was not included, the Support team might simply request it from you to make sure your issue is taken care of in the best way possible.
