# Can I watch replays of my tournaments and championships?

Yes, you can view the shots you and your opponents took for tournaments and championships you have taken part in. To view these replays, navigate to your Results tab and click on any concluded tournament. You can now click on the camera icon, or on any player and then watch replay to proceed to replay mode.
Once inside replay mode, you will be able to toggle between players and their shot attempts to see how each participant played.
