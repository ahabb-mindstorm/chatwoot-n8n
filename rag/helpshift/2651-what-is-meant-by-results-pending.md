# What is meant by "Results Pending"?

Players do not begin tournaments simultaneously.

Tournaments conclude after enough players have joined and completed their games, and you will only view the final results once all players have finished or the overall tournament time has expired.

During this period, the leaderboard will display "Results Pending", indicating that some players have yet to complete their games.
