# How can I change my nickname and avatar?

To change your nickname, follow the steps below:
Tap on "Profile" in the footer or the player avatar pic in the top HUD.
- Tap on your current avatar to open Account details.
- To insert your own nickname, delete the current name and insert a new one.
- Tap on "Apply" to save.
-  To change your avatar, follow the easy steps below:
Tap on "Profile" in the footer or the player avatar pic in the top HUD.
- Tap on your current avatar to open Account details.
- Tap on the Edit button (pencil icon) next to your avatar to see a selection of available avatars, and then tap on one to choose it.
- Selecting an avatar automatically sets it as your new avatar.
