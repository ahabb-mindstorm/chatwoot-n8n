# How Often Are Tournament Courses Updated?

Our tournament course rotation is updated on a bi-weekly basis, bringing in new courses and holes to keep the gameplay fresh, exciting, and challenging. These changes ensure that players experience a variety of courses and new challenges, making each tournament unique and engaging. We are always working to bring more fun and competitive elements to the experience.
