# How do I use my Bonus Cash in tournaments?

Bonus Cash is used to participate in Cash Tournaments.

When you use Bonus Cash to enter a Tournament, you will receive your winning funds as Bonus Cash and as withdrawable funds. For example, if you enter a tournament with $1.00 as the entry fee using Bonus Cash only and win a prize of $2.90, $1.00 will be given back as Bonus Cash, and $1.90 as regular withdrawable funds.
If both regular funds and Bonus Cash are available in your balance, up to 20% of the entry fee will be paid in Bonus Cash upon entering a Tournament, and the rest - in regular funds.
