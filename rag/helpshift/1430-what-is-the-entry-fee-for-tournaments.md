# What is the entry fee for tournaments?

The entry fee is the amount needed to join a tournament. For instance, if a tournament requires a fee of $1 and 600 coins, you must have at least $1 and 600 coins in your balance to enter. The fee is deducted from your account as soon as you join the tournament.
