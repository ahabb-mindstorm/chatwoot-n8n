# How is matchmaking done?

We match players against others of similar skill levels to guarantee an enjoyable and fair experience for all. The matchmaking algorithm improves its accuracy as you play more, learning your skills better over time.

Please be aware that we cannot manually adjust or manipulate the matchmaking process. The algorithm operates entirely automatically. Therefore, we cannot decrease a player's skill level upon request. If such manual control were possible, it would compromise fairness for all players.
