# Can I see the shots my opponents take?

Pro Golf Tournaments provide a real-time & asynchronous multiplayer experience. What that means is that if a player is playing with you in real time you can see his shots and ball positions as he is playing but other players might also compete in this tournament at a different time before its expiry time. In that scenario if they have already played before you we play their shot and score ( leaderboard ) replays while you are playing to enhance multiplayer experience and maintain competitive integrity.
