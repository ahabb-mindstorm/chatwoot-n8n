# Best Shot (Top Shot) rewards

**Best Shot** (players may say **Top Shot**) is a per-tournament bonus for the best shot on a designated hole — separate from **rank/leaderboard** prizes.

## How players earn it

During a tournament, the server tracks best-shot data. If the player wins or qualifies for the best shot prize on that tournament card, a **Best Shot** section appears on the tournament **history / results** heading with a reward.

## Claiming

Best shot is claimed together with or separately from rank rewards when the player claims the tournament result:

- Must not already be **claimed**
- Must not be an **empty** reward
- Must be the **correct user** (best shot winner)

Claim flow grants the reward immediately (currencies/equipment/loot bags) similar to rank prizes.

## Share bonus

If the player **shares** their best shot (social share flow), a one-time **share reward** can be added to **pending rewards** on first share:

- Only if not already `RewardClaimed` for share
- Only for the player who owns that best shot

If they shared but never got the bonus: check if share reward was already marked claimed.

## "Lost" best shot cases

| Situation | Explanation |
|-----------|-------------|
| No best shot row on results | Player did not win best shot for that event |
| Claimed tournament but not best shot | Claim all should claim both; try individual claim |
| Forfeited tournament | Rank prize lost; best shot may also be unavailable depending on event rules |
| Already claimed | Second attempt shows already claimed |

## Distinction from rank prize

Players sometimes confuse **leaderboard cash** with **best shot**. Clarify:

- **Rank** = final position prize
- **Best Shot** = skill shot bonus on a hole

Both can include loot bags — remind player to **open loot bags on home**.
