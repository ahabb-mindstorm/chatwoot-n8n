# What is TopShot?

Top Shot is an exciting new game mode that rewards players not only for their overall performance but also for the difficulty and distance of their shots. Only shots that are taken on hole completion will be eligible for TopShot (may be subject to change in future iterations).
In Top Shot, the player who hits the most challenging shot (calculated based on the distance and difficulty of the shot) will earn a special prize. The difficulty and distance of the shot are considered to make sure that players are rewarded for making impressive plays, not just for finishing a hole. This encourages precision, skill, and strategy in every shot taken.
