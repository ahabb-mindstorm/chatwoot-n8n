# What does XP mean?

“XP” means Experience Points, which you earn by participating in tournaments. These points contribute to filling up the XP meter located at the top center of your game interface. Once the meter is completely filled, you will level up.

As your XP level increases, it requires participation in more tournaments for the percentage bar to advance and for you to achieve the next level.
