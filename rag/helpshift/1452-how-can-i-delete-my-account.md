# How can I delete my account?

To delete your account please send an email at progolf@mindstormstudios.com.
To prevent accidental deletions, our Support team will reach out to you via email to confirm your request.
Once confirmed, we will delete your account.
Please keep in mind that we will not be able to restore your account once it's deleted.
